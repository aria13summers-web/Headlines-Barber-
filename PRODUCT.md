## Users

## Product Purpose

Headlines Barber helps customers discover its grooming services, locations, hours, and contact options so they can arrange an appointment or visit.

## Positioning

A modern barber destination focused on sharp cuts, clean grooming, and confident style.

The supplied announcement also lists Preller Square Shopping Centre, Langenhoven Park, Baysvillage, and Fleurdal Mall; the relationship between these locations and the primary address needs confirmation before launch.

- Tagline: Look Good, Make Headlines.
